The Student Feedback Literacy Instrument

A free, validated questionnaire for measuring how students engage with feedback

SFLI · open access resources and materials

Feedback literacy

Students differ in how well they make use of the feedback they receive. Student feedback literacy names that difference:

the understandings, capacities and dispositions needed to make sense of information and use it to enhance work or learning strategies.
Carless & Boud (2018, p. 1316)

The instrument

We developed a self-report instrument to measure student feedback literacy and validated it with student samples across cultural contexts and languages. The initial instrument was designed and assessed in Woitt et al. (2025). The full SFLI, presented in Weidlich et al. (2025), consists of 22 items across two dimensions: feedback attitudes and feedback practices. The short form, the SFLI-S, consists of 11 items. Both versions are available in English, German, and Turkish.

  • 22items, full version
  • 11items, short form
  • 2dimensions
  • 3languages

You are welcome to use the SFLI in your teaching or research.

Insights from research

For teacher education students, sophisticated feedback worked best when paired with high feedback literacy; without it, the added benefits disappeared compared to simple feedback.
Weidlich, J., et al. (2025). Highly informative feedback using learning analytics: How feedback literacy moderates student perceptions of feedback. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 22(1), 43. Paper · Summary
When comparing teacher, peer, and LLM feedback, university students preferred the LLM’s comments, but their work improved most after teacher feedback. Only those with strong feedback literacy gained this extra benefit.
Weidlich, J., et al. (2025). Teacher, peer, or AI? Comparing effects of feedback sources in higher education. Computers and Education Open, 100300. Paper · Summary

Other instruments

Depending on your purpose, one of these scales may suit you better.

  • Dawson et al. (2025). The Feedback Literacy Behaviour Scale (FLBS) measures feedback literacy with 24 items across five dimensions.

    doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2023.2240983

  • Zhan (2022). This scale measures feedback literacy across six dimensions and 24 items.

    doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2021.2001430

  • Song (2022). The Feedback Literacy Scale (FLS) measures feedback literacy across five dimensions and 21 items.

    doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2022.2042187

  • Yildiz et al. (2022). The Feedback Literacy Scale (FLS) measures feedback literacy across four dimensions and 24 items.

    doi.org/10.35826/ijoecc.624
